A Heritage of Taste

The Legend Behind the Golden Flavor

Makanism ID began in 2010 as a small gathering of passionate culinary experts who shared a single dream: to elevate Indonesian gastronomy to the world stage. Over the past decade, we have transformed from a secret tasting room into a beacon of modern luxury dining.

Our cuisine is a sensory exploration of Nusantara heritage. We take the ancient secrets of spice and smoke, and refine them with cutting-edge techniques of molecular gastronomy. Every plate is a carefully composed masterpiece, a symphony of texture, aroma, and visual poetry.

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Is there a dress code?

Yes, we encourage Smart Casual attire. We request no beachwear or flip-flops to maintain our elegant atmosphere.

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are highly recommended, especially for weekends and dinner service, to guarantee your table.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Please inform your server or note it in your reservation, and our chefs will prepare a meal to suit your needs.
