Cocktail Culture:
A Modern Twist on Classic Beverages

Sep 15, 2025 Mixologist Budi 3 Min Read

"A great cocktail is a conversation in a glass." Mixology has evolved from pouring spirits over ice to a deeply scientific and creative endeavor, pushing the boundaries of what a beverage can be.

At Makanism ID, our bar program is designed to mirror our kitchen's philosophy. We explore the modern twists on classic beverages by introducing unique local ingredients—think pandan-infused rums, kecombrang syrups, and smoked cinnamon bark.

The Chemistry of Taste

Every cocktail we serve is precisely measured, balancing acidity, sweetness, bitterness, and alcohol content. We utilize techniques like fat-washing, clarification, and barrel-aging to extract and refine flavors.

Crafting the perfect balance.

Visual Appeal

Just like our food, the presentation of our cocktails is paramount. From crystal-clear ice carved by hand to the dramatic use of smoke and dry ice, the visual theatricality sets the stage for the first sip.

"We don't just mix drinks; we curate liquid experiences that challenge your expectations and delight your senses."

The next time you visit, take a seat at the bar. Watch the meticulous craft unfold, and discover how a classic recipe can be completely reimagined for the modern palate.
